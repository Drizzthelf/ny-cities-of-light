Set Up Your Account
  1. 01

    Enter the registration code

    The first time you open the app, it asks for a registration code to confirm you're part of the conference. Enter:

    NYCYSACONF
  2. 02

    Sign in with your email

    No password needed — enter your email address and the app sends you a one-time sign-in code.

  3. 03

    Enter the code from your email

    Check your inbox for a 6-digit code and enter it in the app to finish signing in.

  4. 04

    Set up your profile

    Add your first name (required), and a photo or social handles if you'd like — this is what other attendees see when they scan your QR code.

Android
  1. 01

    Join the tester group

    Sign in with a Google account, open the group page, and tap "Join group" (or "Ask to join," if it needs approval) near the top — this is what Google uses to verify you as an approved tester.

    Open the group
  2. 02

    Get the testing link

    Once you've joined, open this thread for the Android External Test link to install the app.
