Cities of Light — Support

Cities of Light (NYC YSA Conference) is a companion app for the conference — a personal QR code for connecting with other attendees, the event schedule, announcements, a raffle, and a leaderboard.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile tab → Settings → Delete account. Without the app installed, use this page.

How do I report another user?
Open their profile (from My Contacts or by scanning their code) and tap Report. Only conference organizers can see reports.

Where's the privacy policy?
Privacy Policy, also available in-app under Settings.
